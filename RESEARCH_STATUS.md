## Later search: no candidate cleared the complete procedure

A separate local campaign completed on October 22 covered 1,589 distinct targets,
with usable real inputs for 1,581. Neither the 1–15 day nor the 0.5–30 day arm
produced a real peak passing the complete statistical procedure. No candidate
advanced to pixel/scene/centroid or FPP promotion. Those unmeasured quantities
are unverified, not zero. No new candidate manuscript resulted.

Independent saved-decision and injected-truth audits pass across 7,220 registered
trials, including unavailable cases. The two period arms share photons and
correlated controls; they are not independent surveys. Recovery is limited to
the tested statistical procedure and does not measure occurrence completeness.
This is an observational negative with limited sensitivity, not evidence that
these stars have no planets. This update makes an aggregate summary public;
the separate campaign's complete code, photon arrays and audit bundle are not
included in this starter repository or independently reproduced by this update.

The campaign source is its dated local report and verification bundle. The
existing public branch reports remain the reproduction sources for the earlier
candidate notes. No new discovery since October 23 is claimed here.

Analysis and publication materials were prepared by AI agents under Ricardo
Pinho's direction. The candidate notes disclose AI use; no expert peer review
or confirmation is implied by a public repository or preprint deposit.
