# Deviations and implementation record

- RULES.md generic requests for live GitHub/arXiv/community searches conflict with the user's narrower MAST/TESScut/Villanova-only network instruction. Those checks remain incomplete; no novelty claim is made. ExoFOP evidence is inherited from the permitted r29 REPORT.md, not refreshed.
- The shared environment lacks Astropy and no offline Astropy wheel was found. No package service was accessed. A job-local venv reuses pinned local NumPy 2.5.4 and SciPy 1.18.1 read-only; a small explicit FITS/TAN reader is retained. This is the protocol's prespecified fallback, not a measurement change.
- TESScut's official GET endpoint returns HTTP redirects to MAST-managed stpubdata.s3.amazonaws.com/tess/public/staged_cutouts/. The first client blocked these as an unlisted host; failed attempts are logged. This delivery prefix is treated as TESScut archive delivery under the user's allowed public GETs to MAST/TESScut. Only a redirect directly returned by an allowed MAST request may reach that exact prefix; arbitrary S3 access remains forbidden. No science threshold or aperture rule changes.
- The launcher already runs at nice 10. An initial additional `nice -n 10` produced nice 20; runtime.json exposed it before scientific measurements. Subsequent commands use `nice -n 0` to retain actual nice 10. Two early download processes were stopped by the machine's scheduler (state T). A SIGINT did not terminate a stopped process and one restart briefly waited on the network lock; both were terminated and confirmed absent before the next launch. No duplicate data request ran concurrently; the serialized network lock remained in force. No TESS flux had been read.
- Header-only implementation detail: Astrocut 1.4.0 omits physical detector WCS from these cutouts. The parent WCS_FFI header is retrieved with a public GET Range request (at most 65536 bytes retained), and its TAN-SIP WCS is inverted to locate the detector PRF grid. The initial unexecuted default-origin code was removed before aperture construction or flux reading. SIP distortion was added to the local reader. No aperture or measurement was changed after flux.
- The scheduler resumed the downloader by 11:49 UTC; no manual override was applied.
- Pre-flux PRF boundary handling: parent-header coordinates place sectors 41 and 82 slightly beyond the PRF grid. Clamp only the PRF interpolation coordinate to its published grid edge (no negative/extrapolated model weights); retain actual catalogue/detector coordinates and all sectors. Clamping distances are saved per sector. The initial header-preparation process was stopped, checked absent, and restarted before any aperture manifest was sealed or field flux read. Earlier unsealed PRF preparation logs are retained. The physical PRF headers (CRPIX=59, CRVAL=CCD_CREF/CCD_RREF, NSAMP=9) confirm the 117x117 sampling and full-FFI RAWX/RAWY convention. No extra 44-column shift is applied: published column grid already starts at 45 in that convention.
- Offline packages were subsequently copied into the job-local venv, retaining exact NumPy/SciPy versions and existing metadata, so measurements load local packages. Source environment remains untouched. See OFFLINE_ENV.md and outputs/runtime_final.json.
- Pre-flux preparation failure (retained in prepare_final.log): the cam4_ccd2 directory contains both `tess-prf-phot-*` and dated SPOC UPDATED_2.0 surfaces. Combining their filename grids made an unavailable corner and raised StopIteration. Filter to the dated `tess201910*` family matching the frozen SPOC UPDATED_2.0 specification. No field flux or aperture manifest had been read/sealed. This is a source-file selection correction, not a changed aperture rule or threshold.
- Missing data handling was implemented before flux: require finite aperture pixels, as frozen, use finite border values for its median, and exclude nonfinite image pixels from PRF fits with their effective degrees of freedom recorded. Empty sectors remain with zero events and injection failures; no sector replacements.
- Kepler comparison was mistakenly launched concurrently with TESS photometry even though its final dilution table requires outputs/photometry.json. It completed all 17 quarter calculations but failed before saving its final file when that dependency was not yet present. The original failure log is retained (kepler_compare.log); after ps confirmed no older comparison process, the unchanged script was rerun after photometry completed. No TESS measurement or score was repeated or overwritten, and no science code changed after its pre-flux seal.

## Material gate limitation discovered after its original outcome

The frozen injection OOE mask excluded only ±0.126 d around both ephemerides' primary and phase-0.5 centers. The task requires out-of-eclipse cadences only. The permitted Villanova overview (downloaded before freeze, widths interpreted after the gate) lists primary pwidth=0.1530 and secondary swidth=0.1384 in phase, with separation=0.5007. At P=3.0305267 d those full widths are 0.4636705851 d and 0.41942489528 d, both broader than the 0.252 d mask. Thus the registered mask does NOT establish strictly out-of-eclipse backgrounds, and can retain EB wings and variability. This was an avoidable protocol-design error. It is not repaired by a post-flux mask/threshold change.

Original outcome retained: target 0/20, EB 14/20; gate FAIL; Bar B UNSCORED. Six trials per position fail already because the measured sector signal is nonpositive or unavailable, so no reassessment of background purity could turn this registered gate into >=18/20 without changing other frozen choices. We do not reinterpret injection positions as source locations and do not rescore Bar B. The saved-output verifier certifies the original numerical decisions, not compliance with the stronger OOE purity requirement. A clean OOE-only injection validation remains incomplete, explicitly reported; no attribution can follow.

Villanova's flags are FB=flat-bottomed eclipses, TM=ETV evidence of a multiple, DV=eclipse-depth variations (sources/vill_about.html). These are prior catalogue context, not discoveries here. The fixed ephemeris was not widened or fit after reading them.
