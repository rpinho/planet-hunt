## D: exact shared robust smoothing

Apply separately per sector to every accepted real series and every injected series BEFORE event fitting. Training excludes the union of the trial's full EB primary and secondary windows and CTOI windows. Define d(t,P,T)=(t-T+P/2) mod P-P/2. EB primary full width=0.1530*PE=0.4636705851 d and secondary full width=0.1384*PE=0.41942489528 d, centered at TE+sector_offset+shift and TE+sector_offset+shift+0.5007*PE. Mask primary |d|<=PE*(0.1530/2+0.05), secondary |d|<=PE*(0.1384/2+0.05), and CTOI |d|<=0.210 d (box half duration 0.105 plus half duration 0.105). Including the secondary is necessary because the full template contains it. No masks are inferred from LC1 flux.

Gaussian local-linear regression: sigma=max(1.4 d,3*primary_full_width)=1.4 d. Thus the Gaussian standard-deviation timescale itself exceeds three primary widths; FWHM=2*sqrt(2*ln(2))*sigma. Grid is sector minimum accepted time + 0.25*j d while strictly below sector maximum, then append the maximum (unique nodes). No cross-sector smoothing. At each grid node g, kernel K=exp(-0.5*((t-g)/sigma)^2), without truncation. Fit y=b0+b1*(t-g) to unmasked training points with weights K*robust_weight/error^2. Initial robust_weight=1. Evaluate trend at all sector times by linear interpolation of grid intercepts.

Perform exactly three Tukey reweight updates and a final fit (four fits total). After each of the first three fits, on training points compute r=flux-interpolated_trend, median m, s=1.4826*median(abs(r-m)); if s>0, u=(r-m)/(4.685*s), robust_weight=(1-u^2)^2 for |u|<1, zero otherwise. If s=0 retain all robust weights=1. No fitting row is removed by these weights. Require >=3 training points and nonsingular weighted local-linear matrices and strictly positive finite trends; any failure is recorded as invalid, never tuned/retried. D returns flux/trend and error/trend. Supplied pre-D errors are unchanged by multiplicative injection; D's estimation uncertainty is not added, and its complete nonlinear response is included in the empirical injection gate.

For the real series shift=0. For injected series use its paired shift in exactly the same D rule, working only on the OOT source timestamps described below. Missing cadences remain missing. Real D and injected D are recomputed from their own flux, never reused from a precomputed real trend.
