# Deviations

1. Catalogue source (before any match was computed). VizieR (cds.unistra.fr and the CfA mirror) dropped every connection on 11-02, so J/AJ/151/68 could not be fetched. Used instead the Villanova Kepler EB Catalog online export (revision 2019-08-08), the maintained version of the same catalogue (Kirk et al. 2016 is its published snapshot). Coordinates there are Galactic (GLon/GLat, 4 decimals). Rows with GLon = -1 have no position and are counted, not matched. Rule, radius, tolerance, harmonics and bar unchanged.
