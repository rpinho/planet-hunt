# Repaired periodic Kepler search — r28

This protocol is drafted during repair on controls and five catalogue-selected known planets. A local freeze commit, immutable PLAN.json, implementation hashes and STATUS.md first line FROZEN will precede the first r28 search of any of the twelve CTOI targets' flux. r27 outcomes are historical baseline only. Nothing is posted, sent to ExoFOP or claimed as a planet. Only public code/data transfers to the named rented pod are authorized. All heavy work runs there.

## r27 batch bar (verbatim)

report the recovered fraction and its denominator against 50 matched controls each. A CTOI is refuted if Kepler coverage >= 0.9 at its period with SNR_expected >= 15 and no event. It is recovered if the folded Kepler signal has z >= 7.1, depth within 3 sigma of the dilution-corrected CTOI depth, and exceeds the 99th percentile of its 50 controls. Anything else is open. Injection recall per target is reported beside each verdict; a refutation needs recall >= 0.9 at the frozen depth.

## Gate (verbatim)

Gate: injection recall >= 0.9 at the frozen depth on matched controls for every target, and >= 4 of 5 known planets recovered. A target whose recall is below 0.9 stays OPEN and is reported as such.

The five-known-planet gate is global. Each target's injection gate applies to both recovery and refutation. A failed target remains OPEN; no threshold adjustment after target flux. Controls and their injection trials may be measured during repair; their final fixed-method results enter the scoring panel. All final control outputs must use the same frozen implementation as targets. No target data are used to select or tune the method.

## Fixed sample, dilution and templates

Reuse r27 FROZEN-v2 PLAN.json target rows verbatim, including Amendment 1's ten corrected durations (24 times CTOI numeric duration in hours), periods, epochs, errors, crowding, dilution, submitter data and 50 ordered control IDs each. The sample is exactly its 12 executable periodic rows; the two missing-duration rows and single-event row are outside this requested batch. No selection from new flux, no duration fitting and no control replacement. PLAN.json retains the source r27 hash and full target rows. r27's catalog snapshots and binary flags remain authoritative for this replication.

Use final Q1–Q17 long-cadence PDCSAP, SAP_QUALITY exactly zero, finite positive flux, fixed BKJD [131.5,1591], no flux clipping. Per-quarter normalization by median; split gaps >0.5 d, segments >=5 points. After dividing by the masked local continuum, multiply the fractional residuals by the frozen reference CROWDSAP to express aperture-equivalent ppm. This division preserves the meaning of the frozen fractional depth under multiplicative injections; subtractive flattening is not used. Expected depth is CTOI depth x (1+TIC contratio) x reference CROWDSAP. PDCSAP is already crowding-corrected. No assumption that bandpass depths are physically identical beyond this registered dilution model.

## Repaired search

Search P +/-5 sigma_P, or +/-0.1% P if sigma_P is absent, without an epoch-based phase restriction. No grid cap. Period separation <= duration*P/(10*1459.5), giving <=duration/10 accumulated drift across the Kepler span, stricter than duration/5. All folded phases are searched with phase separation <=duration/40. Exposure-integrated boxes use LC=0.02043365 d, the single frozen duration, and heteroscedastic weighted least squares with a floating constant. Linear deposition into the phase histogram reduces binning error; raw-time fitting removes it for accepted candidates.

First detrend with a median-initialized, transit-masked quadratic Savitzky–Golay baseline: window max(2 d, 8 durations), nearest allowable odd cadence count per segment. Two iterations mask negative residuals below -3 adjacent-difference robust sigma, dilated by ceil(duration/(2 LC)) cadences, interpolate across masked samples and apply the quadratic SG. After the blind first exhaustive search, additionally mask the strongest fitted periodic box expanded by 2 LC, refit the baseline and repeat the entire exhaustive search. Every independent local maximum with grid z>=7.1, plus the global maximum if absent from that set, is refined on actual cadence times. Local phase maxima are separated by about half a duration; adjacent period cells are compared. No finite number of maxima is retained or discarded. Every retained maximum gets its own periodic transit mask and fit. Bounded L-BFGS-B optimization with analytic derivatives of the exposure-integrated box changes only period and phase within adjacent cells, never duration. A separately checked finite-difference synthetic test validates these derivatives and the raw-time fit statistics. During optimization, cadences outside the union of all possible transit intervals within the bounded cell have identically zero template and derivative, so their contribution is retained exactly through global weight/flux sums; 100 synthetic parameter checks compare the pruned and full statistics. This is exact computational pruning, not a finite event shortlist. Depth and an additive constant are solved analytically. Save strongest and all depth-compatible significant maxima. The strongest matching maximum is selected if one exists, otherwise the strongest maximum.

Noise weights are robust adjacent-difference MAD/sqrt(2), minimum 1 aperture ppm, multiplied by duration-scale robust-scatter inflation >=1, per gap segment. For control injections these weights are fixed from the uninjected input; no amplitude/threshold tuning from injected data. Targets and controls use the identical measurement/search algorithm. In a very short segment with fewer than two surviving baseline samples, retain the initial median baseline; with at least two, interpolate those samples. This prevents masking from silently dropping accepted observations.

Depth gate: |depth - expected| <= 3 sqrt(fit_sigma^2 + (catalogue_depth_sigma*dilution_ratio)^2). Missing catalogue depth uncertainty contributes zero. No approximate depth prefilter.

## Repair validation

Reproduce public Astropy BoxLeastSquares on a synthetic Kepler-span integrated box as baseline, and diagnose r27 with synthetic ablations and control injections, without reading target flux.

100 frozen-depth multiplicative injections per target template into its 50 frozen matched control stars, two per star, before this detrender and after mission PDC. Draw periods uniformly over the repaired search interval and orbital phases uniformly at the fixed Kepler midpoint. Seed = 281102 + target KIC + 1009*ordered_control_index. All injections run the blind all-phase search, with no injection ephemeris supplied to detrending/search. Success needs z>=7.1, the unchanged depth gate, phase localization <=max(duration,LC), and period agreement within twice the grid separation, allowing the adjacent-cell continuous fit. Include all 100 trials in the denominator, including gaps and failures. Save every truth, result, localization and decision. A target below 90/100 remains OPEN. Before target freeze, a separate audit/verify_gate.py imports no measurement code and independently recomputes all 1200 trial decisions and known localizations, using exact rational inequalities on saved decimal measurements (including the squared depth gate).

Five known CONFIRMED KOI signals are selected before flux by nearest log-period/log-depth distance to five target templates, with distinct hosts, using r27's frozen NASA cumulative table. Run on their native unscaled Kepler flux with their own catalogue duration, period and depth, not deeper artificial signals. A known recovery needs z>=7.1 and period/phase localization by the same rule at its catalogue ephemeris; report its native fitted/catalogue depth ratio and depth gate separately because a physical transit's catalogue central depth and a fixed box depth can differ. No known planet is replaced after flux. At least 4/5 must be recovered. This is detection validation, not a claim of realistic transit-shape completeness.

## Coverage and one scoring pass

As r27: predicted centers in BKJD [131.5,1591], count an event covered when accepted exposure overlap >=50% of duration. Save accepted times and nominal, best-fit, and every trial-period coverage counts. Refutation requires nominal coverage >=0.9 and minimum grid coverage >=0.9 at the nominal epoch, plus SNR_expected>=15, no depth-compatible z>=7.1 event, and target recall>=0.9. Preserve r27's conservative requirement for nonmissing epoch and period/epoch errors before refutation; missing ephemeris does not prevent full-phase recovery but prevents refutation. Expected SNR = expected aperture depth*sqrt(weighted box norm) at nominal ephemeris using the masked baseline and frozen noise. Epoch uncertainty is reported but does not restrict search phases.

Exactly fifty control maxima per target, same search. 99th percentile is linear interpolation among 50 maxima; require strict exceedance by target selected z. Report control matching count/50 and rank (1+number control maxima>=target)/51. Recovery takes precedence, then refutation, then OPEN, with validation gates first. Report recovered/12 and gate-passing denominator separately. A mechanical recovery is an archival lead only and never overrides DIAmante binary flags or Boyajian's Star.

Save measurements, gate results, source/implementation hashes and a complete output seal before score.py writes scores.json once (exclusive creation). Independent verify.py imports no measurement code and recomputes depth gates, injection decisions, control percentiles, coverage arithmetic and every verdict from saved outputs, checking the seal and freeze hash. Scientific inference is statistical, not exact astrophysical proof; this verifier certifies saved arithmetic and decisions. Final verdict thresholds, depth tests, coverage fractions and interpolated control percentile comparisons are independently checked with rational/integer inequalities where applicable.
